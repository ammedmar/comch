=================================
Adem coboundaries: the even prime
=================================

This notebook presents the even-prime construction of Adem coboundaries using
the Barratt-Eccles and surjection operads. It evaluates the complete
cochain-level representative, including the non-symmetric corrections, on
standard simplices.
