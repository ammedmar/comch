=========================================
Cartan coboundaries: even and odd primes
=========================================

This notebook presents the even-prime construction first and then its
odd-prime extension following Cantero-Morán and Medina-Mardones. It constructs
Cartan relators in the Barratt-Eccles and surjection operads and evaluates
their chain representatives on standard simplices.

Contents
--------

1. Introduction
2. The Barratt-Eccles Cartan homotopy
3. Table reduction and surjection Cartan homotopies
4. Action on simplices
5. Examples
6. Odd-prime Cartan homotopies
7. The Cartan-relator identity and the odd correction
8. References
